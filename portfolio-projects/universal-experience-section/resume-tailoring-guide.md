## Master narratives

### PayPal — Generative AI Engineer | Aug 2025–Present

Built and evaluated AI-assisted workflows in payments and risk. Segmented fraud model performance to diagnose excess false positives among low-history customers and tuned decision thresholds, reducing false-positive alerts about 32% while holding fraud recall steady. Developed FastAPI / LangGraph dispute workflows with human approval gates, reducing multi-day processing to under four hours. Improved retrieval Precision@5 from 0.76 to 0.89 with hybrid search and reranking on roughly 300 labeled queries. Maintained release-evaluation and monitoring practices for production AI services supporting 99.8% availability.

### Cigna Healthcare — Machine Learning Engineer | Jun 2022–Jul 2024

Developed healthcare predictive models and clinical NLP systems. Fine-tuned transformer models to extract diagnoses and medications from 500K+ deidentified notes, reaching approximately 0.91 F1 and reducing manual chart review by about 65%. Built readmission-risk models using over 2M patient records and 320+ features, with reported 19% fewer preventable readmissions. Produced validated features across 15+ EHR, claims, and laboratory/pharmacy sources. Supported model review with calibration and subgroup testing and deployment with Azure ML / Docker / MLflow.

## Boundaries

- Don't combine separate PayPal achievements into one causal claim: the 32% fraud reduction came from model decisioning; the LangGraph workflow reduced dispute turnaround.
- Don't imply that clinical transformer extraction at Cigna was ambient speech recognition or speech-to-text.
- Don't imply any of the user's *personal* GitHub projects were delivered by PayPal or Cigna.
- Don't claim production deployment for personal portfolio prototypes that run locally only.
- Don't add UMBC research experience to resumes unless explicitly requested.
- Don't make up company-specific role responsibilities to meet a numeric Hiring Manager score. Choose the strongest evidence, identify gaps honestly.
- These metrics are user-provided and not independently verified by public source code. Remove any that cannot be defended or documented.
